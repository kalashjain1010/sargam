Acoustic guitar notes used by Sargam.

These are real recorded steel-string notes from the University of Iowa
Electronic Music Studios (MIS), packaged by tonejs-instruments.

http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

The app loads them from a public copy (jsDelivr). If the same files sit in
this folder as Name.mp3 (E2.mp3, A2.mp3, …), those local files are used first.

Iowa recordings: free to use. Packaged set: CC BY 3.0.
